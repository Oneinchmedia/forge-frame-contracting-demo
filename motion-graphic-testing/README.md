# Motion graphic testing

Exploratory motion work for the One Inch Media brand. Five companion storyboards, all built on the real lockup and all ending on the same logo and line. Nothing in this folder is part of the Forge & Frame site build; the folder is ignored by the Next.js app.

## one-inch-storyboard.html

Storyboard v2 for **ONE INCH**, a thirty-second brand ident built on the one-inch punch, with a seven-second cutdown.

Open the file in a browser. It is self-contained: ten frames with timing, visual, motion, audio and the AI technique each shot proves; a playable animatic drawn live in canvas (Play, scrub, keyboard, and a Full / Cutdown toggle); the proposed palette and type; brand slots for the real logo, colors, fonts, studio scan and voice; the production stack; IP guardrails; and the decisions left to the owner.

The animatic is a previz, not the generated footage the frames describe. The `assets` folder holds the One Inch Media lockup, derived from the primary logo: a reverse (light on black) version for the animatic, the tagline on its own, and light and dark versions of the full lockup for the page header. Type is matched by stand-in Google faces until the real fonts are supplied.

## one-up-storyboard.html

Storyboard v1 for **ONE UP**, the companion piece: the 1 from the mark sprints up a five-floor tower, one Bruce Lee idea and one strike per floor (boards, water, ten thousand, mirrors, the climb), and leaves through the aperture at the top. Same page format: ten frames, a playable animatic with a seven-second cutdown, AI technique per shot, production stack, guardrails and open decisions. Shares the `assets` folder with ONE INCH.

## the-one-storyboard.html

Storyboard v1 for **THE ONE**, a forty-five-second process film: a website built start to finish (audit, the choice, design system, brand learning, design, code, review, QA, deploy, results) through a Matrix lens. The old template site lives in green rain; the brand red is the choice; everything after is in the brand's palette, and the serif 1 is the One. Same page format, ten-second cutdown, AI technique per step, guardrails and open decisions. Shares the `assets` folder.

## one-shot-storyboard.html

Storyboard v1 for **ONE SHOT**, a twelve-second logo opening for the website redesign service, in the shape of the famous gun barrel sequence with every element replaced by the brand's: the barrel is the aperture, the figure is the serif 1 rigged with legs and an arm (Agent 1), the shot is the slash, the blood is the brand red, and the closing iris is the mark. Includes a looping walk, turn and fire cycle for the character, a four-second sting, and the 007-specific guardrails. Shares the `assets` folder.

## the-refresh-storyboard.html

Storyboard v1 for **THE REFRESH**, a forty-second brand film in stick figures about why a brand refreshes: a fort holds a line, time attacks with trends, rivals and years on its flags, the defenders throw old assets, and the refresh arrives as reinforcements led by the mark's 1. The slash sweeps the field and refreshes every stale figure instead of felling it; the wall is rebuilt, the new flag goes up, and the flag becomes the mark. Includes a loop of the two stick-figure kinds, an eight-second cutdown, and the no-weapons, nobody-falls guardrails. Shares the `assets` folder.
